I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), currently serving as a Lecturer in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Sonargaon University, Dhaka.

My academic and research interests span computational fluid dynamics, heat transfer, renewable energy, multiphase and turbulent flows, bubble dynamics, and numerical modelling. My undergraduate thesis used the open-source Basilisk solver and the Volume-of-Fluid method to investigate the dynamics of a rising bubble confined by two vertical walls.

I enjoy connecting engineering fundamentals with practical learning—through undergraduate teaching, student project mentoring, laboratory work, and engineering activities.

Research experience

Understanding bubble motion near confined walls.

Undergraduate thesis · Three-dimensional numerical simulation

RESEARCH 01CFD · Multiphase flow

Rising Dynamics of an Initially Spherical Bubble in Quiescent Liquid Confined by Two Vertical Walls

This research investigates a rising bubble near a vertical corner through 3D numerical simulations. The open-source CFD solver Basilisk and the Volume-of-Fluid (VOF) method were used to simulate bubble motion and examine wall-induced transitions between different motion regimes, offering insight into how confinement affects bubble behaviour.
